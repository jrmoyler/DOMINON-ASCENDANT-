## Content Production Bible — v0.6

**Status:** Canonical art/content production pass following:

1. Game Bible v0.1

3. Combat, Siege & Conquest Bible v0.3

5. 3D Production & Technical Architecture Bible v0.5

> **Every civilization must be recognizable from silhouette, material, motion, sound, and behavior before the player reads a nameplate.**

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This document defines the visual and production identity of all 20 civilizations plus the Axiom Crown.

It covers:

- visual language
- architectural grammar
- district archetypes
- material palettes
- lighting behavior
- environmental language
- construction animation
- damage language
- unit silhouettes
- vehicles
- Leader visual direction
- Founder equipment interpretation
- VFX language
- audio identity
- card-frame system
- Wonder briefs
- modular kit requirements
- art QA rules
- first 50 production assets for the vertical slice

The objective is to prevent the game from degenerating into:

> “the same city with twenty color swaps.”

Each civilization must feel as though it grew from a different philosophy of civilization.

---

# 2. GLOBAL ART DIRECTION

## 2.1 CORE VISUAL TARGET

The world should feel:

- premium
- monumental
- tactile
- inhabited
- technologically advanced
- culturally specific
- readable under tactical play
- cinematic without sacrificing gameplay clarity

Avoid:

- generic cyberpunk
- neon-for-the-sake-of-neon
- random holograms
- floating shapes with no function
- excessive particle fields
- sterile “AI city” aesthetics repeated across factions
- identical silhouettes with different colors
- over-smoothed plastic materials
- implausible architecture that cannot support gameplay

---

# 3. MATERIAL REALISM

All materials should communicate:

- age
- maintenance
- climate
- ownership
- industrial use
- repair history

Even highly advanced civilizations should show:

- seams
- fasteners
- weathering
- dust
- footprints
- heat stress
- water staining
- repaired panels
- localized damage

Technology should feel **built and used**, not generated from glossy concept art.

---

# 4. CIVILIZATION READABILITY

A player should identify a faction from:

1. **Silhouette**
2. **Material**
3. **Motion**
4. **Lighting**
5. **Sound**
6. **Unit posture**
7. **Construction style**

Color is secondary.

---

# 5. CARD FRAME SYSTEM

Every card frame contains:

- civilization crest
- card type glyph
- rarity treatment
- footprint
- cost strip
- key tags
- primary effect
- upgrade indicator

## RARITY VISUAL LANGUAGE

### Common
simple matte frame, minimal ornament.

### Specialized
faction-specific structural detail.

### Elite
layered material and animated micro-detail.

### Legendary
distinct frame architecture and subtle motion.

### Mythic
full-frame environmental treatment.

### Dominion
Relic-linked visual motif unique to conquest rewards.

No rarity should rely solely on brighter color.

---

# 6. CARD ART RULE

Whenever practical:

> **The card art must represent the same asset that the player later places in the world.**

Use high-quality renders from the canonical 3D asset.

This prevents “beautiful card, disappointing 3D object” disconnect.

---

# 7. UNIVERSAL ART QA

Every production asset must pass:

- readable silhouette at gameplay distance
- faction consistency
- valid collision
- valid footprint
- destruction state
- LODs
- gameplay sockets
- no impossible doors/stairs
- no unusable tactical surfaces
- material consistency
- no hidden geometry waste
- proper scale
- correct naming

# 50. FOUNDER HALL

Although not part of the original 50 numbered assets, the Founder Hall is mandatory for the prototype.

It is the first physical anchor of the player's civilization.

Responsibilities:
- deck management
- governance
- campaign planning
- Relic display
- Dominion Doctrine management
- Leader meetings
- city overview

The building evolves visually after each Ascension.

---

# 51. FOUNDER HALL EVOLUTION

Initial:
founding civilization only.

After Forgeweave:
industrial annex appears.

After Eden:
living ecological wing.

After multiple conquests:
architecture becomes increasingly hybrid.

The Founder Hall becomes the clearest physical history of the campaign.

---

# 52. RELIC DISPLAY

Each conquered Relic receives a physical installation in Founder Hall.

Requirements:

- unique silhouette
- civilization material
- subtle ambient behavior
- visible connection to Axiom system

Collecting Relics changes the room.

# 53. ASCENSION ART DIRECTION

Ascension sequence should feel:

- monumental
- ancient
- technological
- culturally specific
- world-changing

Avoid:
- generic beam into sky
- random glowing particles
- treasure-chest reveal language

The Relic should feel like a civilization transferring authority.

---

# 54. FORGEWEAVE ASCENSION

Visual concept:

The Grand Forge powers down.

Molten channels cool.

A deep mechanical tone travels through the city.

The Forge Relic rises from the replication core.

Industrial systems across Ironheart halt.

The Relic travels toward Synara.

At the capital:

a new industrial annex physically assembles into Founder Hall.

Synara materials and Forgeweave materials interlock.

Then the Autonomous Factory blueprint becomes available.

---

# 55. LEADER CINEMATIC RULE

Leaders must be introduced in their civilization's functioning environment.

Do not introduce Daxton standing on a throne.

Introduce him walking a live production floor while making decisions.

Do not introduce Amara standing in a magical forest.

Introduce her managing an actual ecological restoration operation.

Character and system should appear together.

---

# 56. UI VISUAL DIRECTION

- sophisticated command interface

- clear civic dashboard

- excessive glassmorphism
- unreadable translucent panels
- constant animation
- neon cyberpunk HUD language

Each civilization may skin details, but core UX remains consistent.

# 76. ART REVIEW FAILURE CONDITIONS

Reject an asset if:

- it looks like generic sci-fi
- faction identity depends only on color
- the building has no plausible entrance
- tactical cover is accidental/unreadable
- material scale is wrong
- destruction is generic rubble
- card art does not resemble world asset
- construction does not fit faction
- silhouette duplicates another faction
- decorative VFX overwhelm function
