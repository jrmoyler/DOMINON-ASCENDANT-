# DOMINION // ASCENDANT
## Game Bible — Foundation v0.1

> **Every card is a physical asset. Every asset changes your city. Every civilization you conquer changes your deck.**

You begin as one of 20 specialized civilizations, build it from a small settlement into a 3D metropolis, and attempt to absorb the other 19 before challenging the civilization that originally created them all: **The Axiom Crown**.

---

# I. THE WORLD

The planet is known as **Eidolon**.

Centuries earlier, the civilization called the **Axiom Crown** attempted to build a perfectly optimized society. Its governing intelligence concluded that no single civilization could simultaneously optimize every dimension of human existence.

It therefore divided its civilization into twenty autonomous experimental societies.

Each was given one mandate:

- Perfect intelligence
- Perfect community
- Perfect housing
- Perfect science
- Perfect environment
- Perfect biology
- Perfect production
- Perfect ecology
- Perfect robotics
- Perfect connectivity
- Perfect security
- Perfect mobility
- Perfect government
- Perfect finance
- Perfect communication
- Perfect law
- Perfect knowledge
- Perfect logistics
- Perfect exploration
- Perfect long-term civilization

Every experiment succeeded.

And every experiment went too far.

That is the central narrative theme:

> **A civilization's greatest strength eventually becomes its greatest problem.**

---

# II. PLAYER ROLE

You are known as:

# THE FOUNDER

The Founder is simultaneously:

- RPG protagonist
- Head of state
- Military commander
- City planner
- Deck builder
- Explorer

You choose one of the twenty civilizations as your starting faction.

That means a campaign has:

- **1 player civilization**
- **19 rival civilizations**
- **1 final civilization**

Your starting choice dramatically changes the campaign.

# III. THE THREE ECONOMIC RESOURCES

These remain universal throughout the game.

## CAPITAL

Represents material and economic capability.

Generated primarily by:

- Retail
- Industry
- Trade
- Taxes
- Contracts
- Exports
- Conquered territories

Used for:

- Construction
- Land
- Basic cards
- Workers
- Equipment
- Repairs
- Roads
- Utilities
- Commerce

### CAPITAL = WHAT YOU CAN BUILD

## INSIGHT

Represents scientific, technological, and strategic knowledge.

Generated by:

- Laboratories
- Universities
- Discoveries
- Exploration
- Enemy intelligence
- Research quests
- Experimentation

Used for:

- Card upgrades
- Technologies
- Advanced facilities
- Class evolution
- Card fusion
- Research trees
- Advanced units

### INSIGHT = WHAT YOU CAN UNDERSTAND

## INFLUENCE

Represents cultural, governmental, and political power.

Generated by:

- Happy citizens
- Landmarks
- Civic centers
- Leaders
- Culture
- Diplomacy
- Victories
- Festivals
- Conquered populations

Used for:

- Leaders
- Champions
- Territory claims
- Annexation
- Diplomatic actions
- Policies
- Legendary structures

### INFLUENCE = WHAT YOU CAN CONTROL

Together:

> **Capital builds the civilization.  
> Insight advances it.  
> Influence expands it.**

# V. CONQUEST MUST CAUSE A MAJOR EVENT

Every conquest triggers:

# AN ASCENSION

The defeated civilization's central relic rises from its city and travels across the world toward your capital.

Your capital physically changes.

A new district appears containing architecture from the conquered civilization.

Then you receive five rewards.

### 1. Dominion Ability

Their civilization mechanic becomes available.

### 2. Civilization Card Set

Their building cards enter your acquisition pool.

### 3. Class Lineage

Their classes become available to your citizens.

### 4. Leader

Choose what happens to the defeated ruler:

- Recruit
- Governor
- Exile
- Imprison
- Release

Different leaders remember what you did.

### 5. Ascendant Relic

Each civilization possesses one Relic.

Twenty Relics unlock the Axiom Crown.

---

# VI. CONQUEST ALSO MAKES THE GAME HARDER

Every Ascension increases:

# DOMINION PRESSURE

### 3 Civilizations Conquered

Rivals begin fortifying.

### 5 Conquered

Civilizations begin forming alliances.

### 8 Conquered

Enemy champions begin hunting you.

### 10 Conquered

World economy changes.

### 12 Conquered

Civilizations share technology.

### 15 Conquered

Major anti-player coalition forms.

### 18 Conquered

Remaining civilizations enter total-war mode.

### 19 Conquered

The Axiom Crown awakens.

# VII. FOUR METHODS OF CONQUEST

## FORCE

Take the capital militarily.

Fast.

Extremely destabilizing.

## INFLUENCE

Convince enough of their citizens that your civilization is superior.

The government collapses politically.

## ECONOMIC

Gain control of critical resources, businesses, and trade.

Eventually you can purchase or force annexation.

## ALLIANCE

Complete difficult diplomatic objectives and convince the civilization to join voluntarily.

This produces the highest post-conquest Loyalty.

**-Health**

**-Happiness**

- Classes

- Military

The conquest order changes the rest.

**Nocturne → Forgeweave → Automara**

**Concord Vale → Eden Circuit → Hearthspire**

creates an extraordinarily stable population civilization.

The build becomes your political history.

# THE CORE PITCH

> **DOMINION // ASCENDANT is a 3D civilization RPG where your trading-card deck becomes a living city. Every card you play creates a physical building, unit, vehicle, technology, or landmark in the world. Build an economy, develop your citizens, explore rival cities in third person, wage war or conquer through politics and commerce, and absorb twenty competing civilizations. Every civilization you defeat permanently adds its architecture, classes, technologies, leader, and abilities to your own until your city becomes a civilization unlike anyone else's.**
